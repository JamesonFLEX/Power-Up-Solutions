Our story

Making Solar Simple

Power Up Solutions was founded by Andrei and Chase as a dedicated sales organization focused on helping homeowners make the switch to solar. Initially, they contracted out all of the installation work, but over time they noticed a recurring issue: many of the installation companies they worked with weren’t meeting the high standards they wanted for their customers. Determined to provide a better experience, Andrei and Chase decided to build out their own installation team, ensuring that the quality of service matched the level of care they put into sales.

They partnered with industry experts who brought extensive experience in solar installation to the team. Together, they grew the installation side of the business, transforming Power Up Solutions into a full-service company. Today, everything from sales to installation is handled in-house, giving Power Up Solutions complete control over the entire process. This approach ensures customers receive personalized service, superior systems, and a seamless experience from start to finish.
